---
name: Teacher student membership
description: Product rule for which students belong in the teacher's student list.
---

The teacher's student list must include students with booking requests visible to that teacher, even before acceptance. Combine these students with booking and assignment relationships without duplicates.

**Why:** The user explicitly expects a teacher's requesting student to appear as before; a pending request is sufficient, not just an accepted booking.

**How to apply:** Match the teacher's authorized request inbox visibility. Do not expose unrelated students or bypass the restricted access of in-person request tables.
